Project 2

Project Title

Predictive Modeling and Revenue Optimization for eCommerce Product Performance

Data and Source

This project utilizes a structured dataset of eCommerce product performance, including variables related to product characteristics and sales outcomes.

The dataset includes:

  • Product attributes (price, category, ratings, reviews)

  • Sales performance metrics (revenue, units sold)

  • Customer engagement indicators

Sample Size: Approximately 1,000+ product-level observations (based on dataset structure)

Data Preparation:

  • Missing values were identified and handled through filtering and imputation where appropriate

  • Inconsistent variable naming was standardized to ensure accurate analysis

  • Outliers in pricing and revenue were assessed and treated using statistical thresholds

Managerial Problem

eCommerce businesses often struggle to understand which product attributes drive revenue performance. Without predictive insights, marketing and product teams cannot effectively prioritize products, optimize pricing, or allocate promotional resources.

This leads to inefficient decision-making and missed revenue opportunities.

Target Audience

  • Director of Growth Marketing

  • eCommerce Strategy Teams

  • Product and Merchandising Managers

Research Questions / Objectives

  • Which product features have the greatest impact on revenue?

  • How accurately can revenue be predicted based on product attributes?

  • How can predictive insights be used to optimize marketing and pricing strategies?

Expected Impact

This project provides data-driven insights that enable businesses to:

  • Identify high-performing product characteristics

  • Improve pricing and promotional strategies

  • Optimize product positioning and marketing focus

  • Increase overall revenue through predictive decision-making

Methods

  • Linear regression modeling

  • Data cleaning and preprocessing

  • Feature selection and variable analysis

  • Model evaluation (R², error metrics)

  • Data visualization for insight communication

Project 3

Project Title

G4A Baseline Project

Data and Source

The dataset includes:

  • User metrics: 1,000+ active users and ~850+ new users

  • Traffic acquisition: Organic search, direct, referral, social, and paid channels

  • Engagement metrics: Sessions, engagement rate, average engagement time

  • Cohort data: Day 1 and Day 7 retention rates

  • Event tracking: Page views (3,300+), sessions (1,400+), clicks, and form submissions

  • SEO performance: Keyword clicks, impressions, CTR, and average search position

Managerial Problem

The organization is experiencing strong user acquisition but weak retention and zero measurable conversions, making it difficult to evaluate marketing effectiveness and ROI.

Without clear visibility into user behavior, engagement drivers, and conversion pathways, marketing teams cannot:

  • Optimize acquisition channels

  • Improve user retention

  • Drive meaningful business outcomes (registrations, purchases, or sign-ups)

Target Audience

  • Director of Growth Marketing

  • Digital Marketing and Analytics Teams

  • Executive Leadership / Stakeholders

Research Questions / Objectives

  • What channels are driving the most user acquisition and engagement?

  • Where are users dropping off in the engagement and conversion funnel?

  • How effective is the current SEO strategy in attracting and converting users?

  • What factors contribute to low retention and engagement across user cohorts?

Expected Impact

This analysis provides data-driven recommendations to:

  • Improve user retention through lifecycle marketing strategies

  • Optimize high-performing acquisition channels (e.g., organic search)

  • Increase conversion rates through better funnel design and CTA placement

  • Strengthen SEO strategy to capture and convert high-intent users

  • Enable accurate tracking of revenue and key conversion events

Methods

  • Descriptive analytics (user trends, traffic patterns, engagement metrics)

  • Cohort analysis (Day 1 and Day 7 retention tracking)

  • Channel performance analysis (traffic source comparison)

  • SEO analysis (CTR, impressions, keyword performance)

  • Funnel and event analysis (user journey, form completion rates)

  • Segmentation analysis (geographic, demographic, and behavioral patterns)
